## 2. Cooking-game / recipe references

- **Cooking Mama** - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooking_Mama - **READ**. Dish = chain of <=10 s touch minigames (draw lines to chop, flip, timed heat), averaged into bronze/silver/gold; failure shows Mama "fixing it". Lesson: each recipe step is a short self-contained minigame with its own grade, then one medal reveal.
- **Good Pizza, Great Pizza** - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Pizza,_Great_Pizza - **READ**. Toppings unlabeled, recognized by sight; orders range from literal to vague; speed vs accuracy; tips fund upgrades. Lesson: drag toppings by hand onto the base; make ingredients visually distinct enough to need no labels.

- **Dubai Strawberry Cup (viral)** - https://www.sugarandsoul.co/?p=98406 - **SEARCH-RESULT**. Layered strawberries + crunchy filling + hardened chocolate. Lesson: fruit cup = visible clear-cup layers; reward a satisfying "crack/pour" finish step.

## Design takeaways

- Every cooking step gets a **top-center prompt pill with one verb phrase** ("Hold the kettle to pour", "Tap to chop") - MRTGR does this for every step and it carries the tutorial.
- Camera **snaps to a fixed top-down close-up** of the active station; no free camera while cooking.
- Use real gestures: hold-to-pour (fill line), drag-up-to-lift, tap-to-chop, freehand sauce drizzle - not just click-to-advance.
- Keep it forgiving like MRTGR (Serve when ready), but add Cooking Mama-style per-step grades -> one bronze/silver/gold (or "viral %") reveal.
- Burger: doneness gauge with green flip/pull zones + stack score for order and centering (Papa's).
- Toppings: tray of big icon buttons with Clear/Undo/Done; free placement onto the dish (radial on ramen, layered in cup, stacked on bun).
- Reveal shot: oversized hero dish, LIVE badge, fake scrolling comments/likes = the "post it" payoff that ties back to the feed.
- Feed card: full-bleed recipe preview, right action rail (like/comment/share/"Cook it"), bottom-left @creator + caption + trend tag.
- Look: pastel gradient-atlas kitchen, chunky low-poly food with exaggerated layers; size UI generously (top pill and tray must read on phone).
- One hero SFX per recipe (sizzle/slurp/crunch) and clip-friendly framing - MRTGR's Shorts traction came from ASMR-style moments.
